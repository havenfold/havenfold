## What you may do without asking

- Say truthfully that your software is "based on Havenfold", "compatible with the Havenfold protocol" or
  "uses Havenfold", as long as it is clear that your product is yours and not ours.
- Use the names to refer to this project in articles, talks, documentation, package dependency lists and
  academic work.
- Keep the names in unmodified copies of this repository and in the license and copyright notices.

## What needs written permission

- Using any of the names, or a confusingly similar name, as the name of your product, service, company, app,
  domain name or social media account.
- Distributing a modified version under the Havenfold name. Forks are welcome; please give yours a different
  name.
- Using the logos, or suggesting that Eser Teknoloji endorses, certifies or supports your product.
- Claiming "Works with Havenfold" or a similar certification; there is no certification program yet.
