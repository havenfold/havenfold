# Code of Conduct

Havenfold adopts the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/)
as its code of conduct. It applies in every project space (issues, pull requests, discussions, chat) and when
someone represents the project in public.

In short: be kind and patient, assume good faith, welcome people of every background and level of experience,
and criticise ideas, not people. Harassment, insults, sexualised language or imagery, publishing someone's
private information, and sustained disruption are not accepted.

## Reporting

Report a problem to **info@havenfolddev.com** with "Havenfold conduct" in the subject. Reports are read by the project lead and handled confidentially.
If a report concerns the project lead, say so in the subject and ask for it to be handled by another
maintainer.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, issues and other contributions that break this code,
and may warn, temporarily ban or permanently ban a participant, following the enforcement guidelines of the
Contributor Covenant 2.1.
